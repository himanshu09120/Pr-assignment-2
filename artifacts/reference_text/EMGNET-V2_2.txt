• Muscle cells are known as myofibrils 
• They provide actuation by shortening their resting length (contraction)
• Cause: Ratcheting of myosin fiber against the actin fiber 
• Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP) is the fuel for the muscle cells
• Oxygen and Glucose are required to build ATP
• After contraction lactic acid forms and needs to be removed
• Muscle fatigue sets in if this process is not efficient
